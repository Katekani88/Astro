Our process

Six steps from requirement to delivery.

Give us the requirement. We manage supplier identification, specification and quotation.

  1. 1

    Customer submits a requirement

    Send us your product list, specifications or a description of the need.

  2. 2

    ASTRO identifies suitable suppliers

    We source manufacturers and suppliers able to meet the requirement.

  3. 3

    Specifications and quotations are obtained

    We confirm technical details and collect supplier quotations.

  4. 4

    Available options are evaluated

    Options are compared on suitability, cost, lead time and supplier reliability.

  5. 5

    A quotation is presented to the customer

    You receive a clear quotation and recommended way forward.

  6. 6

    Orders and delivery are coordinated

    Once approved, we coordinate the order and delivery.

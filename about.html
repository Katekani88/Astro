About us

About ASTRO

An emerging South African supply and procurement company with ambitions to serve regional and international markets.

Company overview

Connecting industry with the right suppliers.

ASTRO Supply & Services (Pty) Ltd is a South African company focused on mining, industrial equipment, procurement and general supply.

We work between customers and manufacturers, equipment suppliers and distributors, sourcing the products and components operations depend on and managing the process of getting a clear, competitive quotation.

Mission

To simplify industrial supply by sourcing reliable products at competitive terms, backed by professional, responsive service.

Vision

To be a trusted supply and procurement partner for African industry, connecting regional businesses with international manufacturers.

Core values

What guides our work

Integrity

Honest, transparent dealings with customers and suppliers.

Quality

Sourcing with attention to specification and suitability.

Service

Responsive, clear and dependable communication.

Partnership

Long-term relationships built on mutual benefit.

Flexibility

Solutions shaped around the customer’s requirement.

Business approach

We start from the customer’s requirement, not from a fixed product list. That lets us recommend sources and options that actually fit the application.

Procurement capabilities

Supplier identification, specification gathering, quotation comparison and coordination of orders and delivery, described step by step on our Procurement page.

Supplier relationship strategy

We aim to build dependable relationships with manufacturers, wholesalers and distributors, and welcome new partners through our supplier programme.

Commitment to quality

Every enquiry is handled with attention to specification, clear communication and professional service from first contact to delivery.
